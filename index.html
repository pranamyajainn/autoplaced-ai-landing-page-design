    Name, notice period, current CTC. Again. And again. An hour gone before you've applied anywhere.

    You can't tell a strong fit from a long shot, so you apply to everything and hear back from almost nothing.

    Fixed by: fit check
  3. Problem 03

    One resume for every job

    Screening software hunts for each job's must-haves. A generic resume gets filtered out before a human ever sees it.

    Fixed by: tailoring

    Losing track of applications

    Where did you apply, when, and with which resume? That tracker you stopped updating in week two.

    Fixed by: auto-tracking

FAQ

Before you join.

Still curious? Write to hello@autoplaced.ai or ask in our WhatsApp community.

Is the Resume Builder free?

Yes. Build one resume, check its ATS score and export it on the Free plan. Paid plans add more resumes and a tailored version for every job.

Will it make things up on my resume?

Never. It reorders and rephrases what is already on your profile. It will not add skills or roles you do not have.

Can recruiters tell an AI applied?

They see you: your real name, your own resume, your answers. Applications go out at a human pace with daily limits, not hundreds at once. Anything it isn't sure about waits for you.

Will my job site accounts get blocked?

Autoplaced never bypasses CAPTCHAs and works at a human pace, with daily caps and cool-downs. Recruiter and LinkedIn notes are drafts, sent only when you say so.

What happens to my data and logins?

Your login details are stored encrypted, and your data is never used to train AI.

Will it apply to every job it finds?

No. It scores every role against your profile, and only roles above your score threshold make the list. In Approve-first mode, nothing goes out without your OK.

Which platforms are supported?

Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters, Naukri, Wellfound, Instahyre, Indeed, Foundit and major remote boards, with more added regularly.

Can I control the automation?

Choose Manual, Approve-first or Full auto. Set roles, locations, salary and seniority, cap daily volume, and pause anytime.

What does it cost after the free applications?

The free plan stays free. Paid plans launch soon, and founding members pay half, for life.

Can I cancel a paid plan anytime?

Yes. Plans are monthly and you can cancel whenever you like. Your resumes and history stay available on the Free plan.
